ROTORCRAFT
Helicopter flight simulator, version {VERSION}

Learn to fly helicopters at Kinloch on the Scottish coast: from your first lesson in the
Kestrel to offshore crew changes, air ambulance, police, military and search-and-rescue
flying. Or take a helicopter out in freeplay, or run your own operator.


STARTING
  Rotorcraft.exe      The game. Full screen; change it in Settings.
  Start in VR.bat     The game in a PC VR headset. Connect the headset first (a Quest over
                      Link or Virtual Desktop, or any OpenXR headset), then start this.

NEEDS
  Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and a graphics card that runs DirectX 12.
  A joystick, gamepad, rudder pedals or VR headset are all optional.


CONTROLS (every key can be changed in Settings)
  W / S             collective up / down (hold Shift for fine)
  Arrow keys        cyclic
  A / D             pedals
  E                 engine start / stop
  R / F             throttle open / idle
  T / U             trim the cyclic and pedals / release the trim
  B N O L           beacon, position lights, strobes, landing light
  K                 switch panel        H   searchlight
  1 2 3             autopilot: altitude, heading, hover hold (twin-engine types)
  V X J             radio, winch, thermal camera
  C                 camera              I   instrument panel      M   map
  Tab               crew tablet (freeplay)                         P or Esc   pause

  Gamepad: right stick cyclic; left stick collective (up and down) and pedals (across);
  A engine, X camera, Y trim, RT release trim, LB / RB throttle, LT tablet, Start pause.
  In the menus the D-pad or left stick moves, A selects and B goes back.

  Phones and tablets (web version): on-screen lever, pedals and stick.


YOUR SAVES
  Kept on this computer in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Rotorcraft\Rotorcraft\Saves.
  Settings > Career save exports a backup you can import on another computer. Signing in
  with Google (main menu) carries your career between the Windows and web versions.
