A whole flying career

Kinloch is a stretch of Highland coast with towns, glens, an airfield and the North Sea beyond it. Everything in it is there to fly to.

Learn, then earn

Enrol at the Kinloch Rotor School. Work through 12 ground-school modules and 8 exams to a private licence and then a commercial one, with night and instrument ratings and type ratings on the twins. After that, seven employers have work for you.

A whole Highland region

Towns, glens, castles, a distillery, a harbour and a lighthouse. There are 88,000 trees, hedges and drystone walls, traffic on the roads, people in the streets, and fishing boats and gas platforms out at sea.

Weather that changes the job

Rain, gusts, coastal haar and real cloud layers to climb through. Fly by day, at dusk or at night, with lit towns, landing lights, searchlights and a thermal camera.

A flight model that means it

Ground effect, vortex ring, blade stall and autorotation. All five cockpits are live, with working instruments, a walk-round and a real start-up. Choose Beginner, Standard or Realistic handling, and use trim and autopilot when you need them.

Freeplay and your own company

Pick a helicopter, fit the kit the job needs and take random callouts, or start an operator of your own: buy aircraft, win contracts and fly the work.

Fly it in VR

Quest over Link or Virtual Desktop, or any OpenXR headset. Reach out and take hold of the cyclic and collective, and press the switches with your fingertip.

Get Rotorcraft

It's free. Download it for Windows, or play straight away in your browser. Sign in with Google from the main menu to carry your career between the two.

Download Rotorcraft
  1. Unzip it anywhere you like. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All.
  2. Run Rotorcraft.exe. The game isn't code-signed, so Windows may say it "protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. For VR, connect your headset first (a Quest over Link or Virtual Desktop, or any OpenXR headset), then run Start in VR.bat.
  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and a graphics card that runs DirectX 12.
  • Keyboard and mouse are enough. A joystick, gamepad, HOTAS, rudder pedals or VR headset are all optional.
  • Saves stay on your computer. No account is needed.

Questions

Is it really free? Yes. It has no adverts, no purchases and no tracking.

Do I need to be online? Only to sign in. The Windows version flies offline.

Mac or Linux? Play the browser version.

Does it show on Discord? Yes, in the Windows version. If the Discord app is running, your profile shows what you're flying. You can turn it off in Settings.

Are the licences real? No. The qualifications are part of the game and carry no real-world privileges.

Where are my saves? On Windows they're in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Rotorcraft\Rotorcraft\Saves. In Settings, Export save makes a backup.
