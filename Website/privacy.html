Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. This covers the Windows game, the web version and this website.

In short: you can play the whole game without an account, and then nothing about you leaves your device, apart from the playing status the Windows game gives the Discord app if you use it (you can turn that off). Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, your save goes into a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, and your pilot name and flying statistics appear on the shared leaderboards. You can hide them at any time. Other players never see your email address or your Google profile. There are no adverts, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing is sold.

Who we are

Rotorcraft ("the game", "we") is a free, non-commercial helicopter simulator made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. For anything about this policy or your data, email ddavies5824@gmail.com.

Playing without signing in

Your career, freeplay records and settings stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Windows: they're kept in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Rotorcraft\Rotorcraft. Deleting that folder deletes them.
  • Web version: they're kept in your browser's storage for this site. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them.

In Settings, Export save makes a backup file you can keep or import on another computer.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional: main menu › Login › Sign in with Google. On Windows, Google's own sign-in page opens in your web browser and passes the result back to the game through a connection on your own computer. The game keeps a sign-in token in its save folder on your computer, so you stay signed in. On the web, Google's sign-in window opens instead. Either way, the game never sees your Google password. Google asks your permission for these:

PermissionWhat the game does with it

Discord status (Windows)

If the Discord app is running on your computer, the Windows game tells it what you're doing so Discord can show it on your profile: the screen or flight you're on, the current task, the aircraft, your in-game rank and flying hours, and how long you've been playing. The game hands this to the Discord app on your own computer, not to us. It doesn't read your Discord friends or messages and keeps nothing from Discord. Who sees the status is up to your Discord settings (User Settings › Activity Privacy) and Discord's own privacy policy. The web version never does this.

Why we use your data

  • To let you carry your save between devices, including between Windows and the web (your own Google Drive).
  • To show the leaderboards and the pilot directory you take part in.
  • To keep the service working and stop misuse, for example by letting each player change only their own profile.

Under UK data protection law, we rely on your consent when you sign in and choose to appear on the leaderboards, and on our legitimate interest in running the game's online features safely. We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or anything else, and we never sell or share it.

Services the game relies on

  • Google provides sign-in, your Drive for saves, and Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for the pilot area. Google processes this data for us under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Netlify hosts this website and the web version: netlify.com/privacy. The Windows download may be served by GitHub: GitHub's privacy statement. Like any web host, they briefly log technical details such as your IP address in order to deliver the files.
  • Discord shows your playing status on Windows, only if you have the Discord app running: discord.com/privacy.

The game and this website have no adverts, analytics, tracking cookies or third-party trackers. The typefaces are served with the site, not fetched from a font service.

How long we keep it

  • Your public pilot profile stays until you hide it, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Your sign-in account stays until you ask us to delete it. Accounts unused for two years may be deleted along with their profile.
  • The save file in your Drive is yours. It stays until you delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Hide yourself from the leaderboards: go to main menu › Login and untick Show me in the pilot directory and on the leaderboards. This deletes your public profile straight away.
  • Stop showing your status on Discord: untick Show what I'm flying on Discord in the game's Settings, or turn activity sharing off in Discord.
  • Sign out: main menu › Login › Sign out.
  • Remove the game's access to your Google account: go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Rotorcraft.
  • Delete the save in your Drive: in Google Drive, open Settings › Manage apps, find Rotorcraft, then choose Options › Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete your sign-in account and everything about you: email ddavies5824@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We'll delete your account and profile within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

You also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected, or to object to how it's used. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Anyone can read the public pilot list, but the database's rules let only you create, change or delete your own profile, and nothing else in it can be read or written. No method is perfectly secure, but we only keep what the features need.

Children

The game itself is suitable for all ages, but the online features (Google sign-in and the leaderboards) aren't intended for children under 13. A parent manages Google accounts for under-13s through Family Link. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, email us and we'll delete their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses data, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
